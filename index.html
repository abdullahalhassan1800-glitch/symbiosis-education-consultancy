    Our process

    From first call to confirmed seat in four steps

    Start with step 01 →
    01.

    Counselling & profile evaluation

    A focused session on your marks, NEET score, budget and city. We tell you honestly what fits.

    02.

    College shortlisting

    We compare recognised colleges, year-wise fees, hostel and mess costs, so you can decide with numbers in front of you.

    03.

    Documentation support

    Marksheets, ID and photos checked. A one-time registration (₹5,000 – ₹10,000, depending on the programme) holds your seat.

    04.

    Admission confirmed

    Fees go directly to the institution. We stay with you before, during and after admission.

    Guidance is advisory. Admission is subject to each institution's own eligibility, counselling and seat-allotment rules — it is never guaranteed.

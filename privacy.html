1. Who we are

Symbiosis Education Consultancy ("we", "us") provides education admission guidance in India and overseas. Contact: +91 82006 91036.

2. What we collect

  • Details you enter in the booking form: name, phone/WhatsApp number, current qualification, course of interest, NEET score (optional), city, meeting preference and any notes.
  • Basic, anonymous usage analytics, only if enabled.

We do not ask for payment card data, government ID scans, or medical records through this website.

3. How the booking form works

This website has no server-side database. When you submit the form, your details are packaged into a WhatsApp message and opened in your own WhatsApp app, addressed to our business number. Nothing is stored on this website. Delivery and retention then follow WhatsApp's own policies until we receive your message.

4. How we use your information

  • To respond to your counselling request and confirm a slot.
  • To advise on courses, colleges and admission steps you ask about.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the institution(s) you choose to apply to, and only with your knowledge, as part of the admission process.

6. Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your admission enquiry and any applicable legal requirements. You may ask us to delete your details at any time.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by messaging us on WhatsApp or calling the number above.

8. Children

Our services are intended for prospective students and their parents/guardians. Where the student is a minor, a parent or guardian should submit the enquiry.
